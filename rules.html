City Highway Code

Local Cycling Laws & Etiquette

Switzerland and European cities maintain strict, well-regulated cycle infrastructure. Keep yourself safe and legal with these essential guidelines.

•

Separated Cycle Tracks

Always use marked red cycle lanes or separated bike paths where available. Riding on pedestrian sidewalks is prohibited by law.

•

Automatic LED Lighting

All Velo City bicycles feature hub-dynamo front and rear LED lights that remain illuminated automatically dusk till dawn.

•

Tram Track Crossing

When traversing tram rails on street junctions, always cross at a sharp right angle to avoid catching your wheels in track grooves.

•

Strict Zero-Alcohol Limit

The municipal legal blood alcohol limit for operating standard and electric assist bicycles matches strict automotive regulations.

Standard Rider Agreement (Summary)

Standard Terms

1. Authorization to Ride: The customer confirms they are at least 16 years of age (or 14 with an approved moped permit for electric assistance models) and physically capable of safely operating a two-wheeled vehicle in urban traffic.

2. Deposit & Payment: Velo City requires no initial credit card deposit hold. Standard rental charges and selected insurance additions are processed upon reservation confirmation.

3. Maintenance & Condition: Every bicycle is checked and sanitized prior to dock release. The rider agrees to inspect tires and brakes before departing the station dock and report any defects immediately.

4. Anti-Theft Responsibility: When temporarily leaving the bicycle unattended outside station docks, the rider must engage the supplied ABUS Bordo lock to an immovable anchor. Unlocked theft voids coverage.

Agreed electronically upon QR pass creation. Accept & Choose Bicycle
